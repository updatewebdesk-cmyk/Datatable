Google Sheet Connection Guide

To view a sheet natively here, complete these 3 rapid steps:

1

Open your Google Sheet and click the big blue Share button on the top-right.

2

Under General access, change "Restricted" to "Anyone with the link" and ensure the role is set to Viewer.

3

Copy the sheet's URL from your browser's address bar and paste it in the configuration box to the left.

🔒 Privacy Information: Your document is completely fetched and aggregated client-side. No spreadsheet data is stored or transmitted to external servers.
